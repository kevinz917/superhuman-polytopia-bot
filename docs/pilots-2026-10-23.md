# Three-hour pilots (2026-10-23)

Six ~3-h pilots launched 16:48-16:52 PT, each on 1×H100 + 8 actor containers (cpu=16, 32 processes); re-planned at
17:22 to a $300 total: A3 (adaptive entropy), B2 (frozen pool) and B3 (water-heavy maps) were stopped after ~35 min.
Ledger `runs/pilots-1023` (~$245 committed at billed-rate estimates). Evaluation on proposed-v1 maps, every map in
both seats, deterministic both sides.

**Results: the low-LR polish (B1) gives a new best policy, +41 Elo over the champion. A smaller batch (A2) learns at
the same rate per sample. The bigger network (A1) is ahead at equal samples in decided games but got too few
samples in 3 h to judge.**

| pilot | change | result |
|---|---|---|
| **B1 polish** | champion scratch-cont-2 + its critic, LR 3e-4 → 1e-4, 2 epochs | **286-222-4 vs the champion (55.9% [52.7, 58.8], ≈ +41 Elo)**, 512 seat-paired games |
| A2 batch 65k | from scratch, batch 262,144 → 65,536 (max_lag 40) | at equal samples (1.11B) vs Scratch 7h u4250: 62-66 (48% [41, 55]) = parity |
| A1 bigger net | from scratch, 4 blocks × 128 (1.97M params vs 519k), minibatch 4096 | at equal samples (~325M) vs Scratch 7h u1250: 13-1 in decided games, 114 of 128 games capped |
| A3 adaptive entropy | entropy target 3.3 → 2.3 | stopped at ~31 min (re-plan) |
| B2 frozen pool | 30% vs OMNI c3, c3, Scratch 7h, Scratch-cont 6h | stopped at ~35 min (re-plan); was beating all four |
| B3 water maps | water types doubled | stopped at ~35 min (re-plan) |

## Detail

- **B1** trained 2,886 updates at ~72k samples/s (learner-bound: two passes per batch). Against its own recent
  snapshots it looked flat (normal at a low LR); the 512-game final is the measure. The final match played
  `latest.npz` at update 2,850; `final.npz` (update 2,886) is archived at
  `/retained/checkpoints/distributed-final-v1/scratch-polish-b1-final/`. Its own fog-of-war value head is untrained
  (omniscient-critic training), so serving it with search needs a fog head trained onto it
  (docs/scratch-search-2026-10-23.md) or a borrowed head.
- **A2** ladder vs c3 / OMNI: update 7,800 (511M samples) 42% / 27%; 12,800 (839M) 66% / 33%; 16,800 (1.10B)
  69% / 44%. Learner-bound at ~103k samples/s: per-update fixed costs (value pass, dispatch) do not shrink with
  the batch. No sample-efficiency gain at this scale; keep 262k.
- **A1** ran out of GPU memory at update 190 (16 GB allocation in the training step, minibatch 8192) two minutes
  after 8 extra actor containers connected; resumed from update 150 with minibatch 4096 and the original 8 actors.
  Actor-bound at 27-38k samples/s (the network costs ~7× more per move on the actors' CPUs), so it reached only
  ~321M samples. Ladder vs c3/OMNI stayed at 2-5% through update 1,150, matching Scratch 7h at the same samples.
  In the matched match most games hit the 100-round cap (both policies are still early-stage), but the decided
  games went 13-1 to the bigger net. Suggestive, not conclusive: the capacity question needs ~2-3B samples, which
  needs actor inference on GPUs or far more actor CPU (the Curio cluster).
- **Bottlenecks:** A1 actor-bound (learner idle ~2-6 s per 7-8 s update); A2 learner-bound (0.64 s per 65k update);
  B1 learner-bound by design (3.7 s training per batch), dropping ~260 stale messages per update.

## What to do with it

- Polish phases work: decay the learning rate (and/or add a second epoch) at the end of every long run.
- Keep batch 262,144.
- The bigger network deserves a proper test at 2-3B samples, which needs GPU actor inference or the Curio cluster.
